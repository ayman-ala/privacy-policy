BeityCare Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

العربية
Summary: BeityCare uses personal information to operate its home-services marketplace, arrange services, support communication and payments, send notifications, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

The controller of the personal information described in this policy is Ayman Ala, operating BeityCare (“BeityCare”, “we”, “us” or “our”). BeityCare provides a platform that connects clients with participating home-service suppliers. This policy applies to the BeityCare website and mobile applications for clients, suppliers, and administrators.

Privacy questions and requests concerning personal information may be sent to privacy@beitycare.com. General support questions may be sent to support@beitycare.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, mobile number, account role, preferred language, and optional email address.
  • Service information: requested service, address, location coordinates, notes, access instructions, and photographs you choose to upload.
  • Activity information: requests, quotations, appointments, messages, cancellations, refunds, invoices, and service history.
  • Payment information: payment status, amounts, and transaction references. BeityCare does not store complete payment-card credentials.
  • Supplier information: business or legal name, optional identification details, phone number, address, coverage areas, agreed commissions, and submitted invoices.
  • Technical information: device and platform information, app version, push-notification tokens, language settings, and security or diagnostic data.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; match clients with suppliers; manage requests, visits, quotations, messages, payments, invoices, cancellations, and refunds; send service and security notifications; provide support and resolve disputes; prevent fraud and misuse; comply with accounting, tax, and legal obligations; and improve the reliability and usability of our services.

4. How we share information

We share only what is reasonably necessary with the client or supplier assigned to a request and with service providers that help us operate the platform, such as hosting and database services, mapping services, push-notification providers, communications providers, and payment partners. These may include Supabase, Google/Firebase, Apple/APNs, Google Maps, Meta/WhatsApp when enabled, and OMT or Whish where available. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganization. We do not sell personal information.

5. Location, photos, chat, and notifications

Location is used to identify the service address and assist service delivery. Photos are used to explain a service request and may be visible to the assigned supplier and authorized BeityCare personnel. In-app messages support coordination and may be retained for support, safety, and dispute handling. Device tokens are used to deliver push notifications. You can control device permissions through your operating-system settings, although disabling them may limit features.

6. Retention and account deletion

We apply the following standard retention periods. A record may be kept longer only when reasonably necessary for an unresolved service, payment, refund, complaint, fraud or safety investigation, legal claim, court order, or legal, accounting, or tax obligation. When the applicable period ends, we delete the information or irreversibly anonymize it.

  • Account and profile information: retained while the account is active. After a confirmed deletion request, account access is deactivated immediately and information that is not required for an obligation below is deleted or irreversibly anonymized within 30 days.
  • Telephone verification records: one-time codes expire shortly after issue; verification challenge and attempt records are deleted within 30 days.
  • Security and access records: retained for up to 12 months, unless needed longer to investigate suspected fraud, abuse, or a security incident.
  • Service requests: retained while active. Closed requests, quotations, appointments, cancellations, ratings, and complaint outcomes are retained for up to 5 years after closure, except when part of a financial record covered by the 10-year period below.
  • Chat messages and photographs: retained while the request is active and for up to 24 months after it closes. Following account deletion, they are deleted or de-identified within 30 days unless a specific item is required for an unresolved complaint, safety matter, fraud investigation, or legal claim.
  • Invoices, payment, and refund records: invoices, transaction references, supporting quotations, refunds, commissions, supplier settlements, tax records, and associated accounting evidence are retained for 10 years from the end of the year to which the record relates. Access is restricted, and direct identifiers are removed or masked where they are not legally required.
  • Supplier business and compliance records: supplier agreements, billing identity, commission terms, and records needed for accounting, tax, payment, or legal obligations are retained for up to 10 years after the relevant relationship or transaction ends.
  • Notification tokens: retained while the account and device registration remain active. They are disabled when deletion begins and removed within 30 days, or earlier if invalid.
  • Support communications: retained for up to 3 years after the matter is closed.
  • Deletion records: a minimal record of the request, identity verification, dates, and completion status is retained for 5 years after completion.
  • Backups: information removed from active systems may remain in restricted backup copies until overwritten by the normal backup cycle, for no more than 90 additional days.
Delete your account in the iPhone app: a client or supplier can start permanent deletion from Account → Delete account. The consequences are shown and confirmation is required. Confirming deactivates access immediately as the first security step and begins permanent deletion; deactivation is not the final outcome. BeityCare normally completes deletion within 30 days and provides confirmation when complete.

An active service, amount due, pending refund, or dispute is not erased by deleting an account. BeityCare may retain only the information needed to resolve that matter or meet a legal obligation. If this requires more than 30 days, we will inform the user. No new service requests may be placed after deletion begins.

Emailing privacy@beitycare.com remains an optional way to obtain assistance or exercise another privacy right, but users are not required to send an email to initiate deletion from the iPhone application.

7. Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure. Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to appropriate contractual and legal protections.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You may update some account information directly in BeityCare. To delete an account from the iPhone application, use Account → Delete account. For other requests, contact privacy@beitycare.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Children

BeityCare is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@beitycare.com.

ملخص سياسة الخصوصية

يمكن للعميل أو المورّد بدء الحذف الدائم من تطبيق iPhone عبر الحساب ← حذف الحساب. يؤدي تأكيد الطلب إلى تعطيل الوصول فوراً كخطوة أمنية وبدء عملية الحذف الدائم، ولا يُعد التعطيل بديلاً عن الحذف. تُحذف بيانات الحساب غير الواجب الاحتفاظ بها، أو تُزال هويتها بصورة غير قابلة للرجوع، خلال 30 يوماً عادةً، ويُرسل تأكيد عند اكتمال العملية.

تُحفظ سجلات التحقق لمدة تصل إلى 30 يوماً، وسجلات الأمن لمدة 12 شهراً، والمحادثات والصور لمدة 24 شهراً بعد إقفال الطلب، وسجلات الطلبات المقفلة لمدة 5 سنوات، والسجلات المالية والفواتير والدفعات والمبالغ المستردة لمدة 10 سنوات من نهاية السنة المعنية. يُحتفظ بمعلومات الدعم لمدة 3 سنوات، وبالحد الأدنى من سجل طلب الحذف لمدة 5 سنوات. قد تبقى النسخ المحذوفة في النسخ الاحتياطية المقيّدة لمدة لا تتجاوز 90 يوماً إضافياً.

قد نحتفظ بسجل محدد لمدة أطول عند وجود طلب مفتوح أو دفعة أو استرداد أو نزاع أو تحقيق أمني أو موجب قانوني أو محاسبي أو ضريبي. في هذه الحالات نقيّد استخدام السجل ونحذف أو نحجب البيانات التعريفية التي لا يلزم الاحتفاظ بها. للمساعدة أو لممارسة حق آخر، يمكن التواصل عبر privacy@beitycare.com.
