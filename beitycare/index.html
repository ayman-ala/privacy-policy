BeityCare Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026

العربية
Summary: BeityCare uses personal information to operate its home-services marketplace, arrange services, support communication and payments, send notifications, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

BeityCare (“BeityCare,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a platform that connects clients with independent technicians and service companies (“suppliers”) for home-service requests. This policy applies to the BeityCare mobile applications, website, and related services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, mobile number, account role, preferred language, and optional email address.
  • Service information: requested service, address, location coordinates, notes, access instructions, and photographs you choose to upload.
  • Activity information: requests, quotations, appointments, messages, cancellations, refunds, invoices, and service history.
  • Payment information: payment status, amounts, and transaction references. BeityCare does not store complete payment-card credentials.
  • Supplier information: business or legal name, optional identification details, phone number, address, coverage areas, agreed commissions, and submitted invoices.
  • Technical information: device and platform information, app version, push-notification tokens, language settings, and security or diagnostic data.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; match clients with suppliers; manage requests, visits, quotations, messages, payments, invoices, cancellations, and refunds; send service and security notifications; provide support and resolve disputes; prevent fraud and misuse; comply with accounting, tax, and legal obligations; and improve the reliability and usability of our services.

4. How we share information

We share only what is reasonably necessary with the client or supplier assigned to a request and with service providers that help us operate the platform, such as hosting and database services, mapping services, push-notification providers, communications providers, and payment partners. These may include Supabase, Google/Firebase, Apple/APNs, Google Maps, Meta/WhatsApp when enabled, and OMT or Whish where available. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganization. We do not sell personal information.

5. Location, photos, chat, and notifications

Location is used to identify the service address and assist service delivery. Photos are used to explain a service request and may be visible to the assigned supplier and authorized BeityCare personnel. In-app messages support coordination and may be retained for support, safety, and dispute handling. Device tokens are used to deliver push notifications. You can control device permissions through your operating-system settings, although disabling them may limit features.

6. Retention and account deletion

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, including legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements. You may request account deletion or access to your information by emailing privacy@beitycare.com. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

7. Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure. Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to appropriate contractual and legal protections.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw optional permissions and change notification settings on your device. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children

BeityCare is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@beitycare.com.
