Delete your BeityCare account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Before you request deletion: Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your BeityCare account, requests and account history.

How to submit your request

  1. Use the Request account deletion button in the Account section of the BeityCare app; or
  2. Email support@beitycare.com from an address available to you.
  3. Include your registered name, phone number and whether your account is a Client or Supplier account.
  4. BeityCare may contact you to verify that you own the account before completing deletion.

Email deletion request

What BeityCare deletes

After verification, BeityCare will delete or anonymize the account and associated personal information that is no longer required, including profile information, device-notification tokens and user-provided content where deletion is legally and technically permitted.

Information that may be retained

Limited transaction, invoice, payment, security, fraud-prevention or dispute records may be retained when required by applicable law or for legitimate legal obligations. Retained information will remain protected and will not be used for unrelated purposes.

Need help?

Contact support@beitycare.com. You can also review the BeityCare Privacy Policy.
